Install openglix apps

openglix apps are free, offline-first, and distributed as direct APK downloads. No Play Store account is required.

  1. Download the APK: Open the APK Downloads page and tap the download button for your app.
  2. Open the file: Tap the downloaded file from your notification bar or Files app.
  3. Allow installation: Android may ask to allow “Install from unknown sources” for your browser. This is normal for APKs outside the Play Store.
  4. Install & open: Tap Install, then Open. If Play Protect warns you, choose “Install anyway” only for APKs you trust from this site.

FAQ & Help

Is it really free?
Yes. openglix apps are free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

Does it need internet?
No. The apps work offline. You only need internet to download the APK or optionally upload a backup.

Where is my data stored?
On your phone, in the app’s private database. Data is not sent to openglix servers.

How do I update?
Download the new APK from this site and install it over the old version. Your existing app data is normally kept.

About openglix

openglix builds small, practical, offline-first Android apps. The goal is simple: useful software that respects your privacy and does not force accounts or subscriptions.

  • Offline-first: Your data stays on your device.
  • Private: No tracking or cloud dependency.
  • Free: No ads and no premium tiers.
  • Direct: APK downloads and clear release notes.
