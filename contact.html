Before you email

  • Check the FAQ & Help page — installation, offline use, updates, and privacy questions are already answered there.
  • Looking for the latest version? All current APKs are on the APK Downloads page.
  • App not working? Include your phone model, Android version, and app version — it makes debugging much faster.
  • Time-sensitive issue? Add “URGENT” to the subject line so it stands out.

Response times

To keep every reply accurate and useful, each message is reviewed by a real person before we respond. Here's a rough guide to expected response times:

  • Support emails — usually 4–8 hours.
  • Bug reports — acknowledged within a day, fixed based on severity.
  • Custom development inquiries — replied within 1–2 working days with next steps.
