FAQ & Help

Everything you need to know about installing, using, and updating openglix apps — search below or pick a category.

31 questions
What is openglix?

openglix is a small independent project that builds practical, offline-first Android apps — the main one being booksXR, a business finance tracker. Apps are distributed as direct APK downloads, with no Play Store account required.

Are the apps really free?

Yes. Every openglix app is free — no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no premium tiers. There are no hidden costs.

Do I need an account to use the apps?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account creation. Apps work as soon as they're installed, and all data stays on your device.

What apps are currently available?

The current list is on the APK Downloads page, and it's also shown at the bottom of this page. Each card shows the version, file size, release date, and a changelog.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer is recommended. Older versions may work but are not officially tested. All openglix apps are Android-only — there is no iOS version at this time.

How do I install an APK?

Four steps:

  1. Tap Download APK on the app card. The file saves to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notification bar or Files app.
  3. If Android asks, allow Install from unknown sources for your browser.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
What does "Install from unknown sources" mean?

Android blocks APK installs from outside the Play Store by default. To install an openglix app, you need to allow your browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) to install unknown apps. This is a one-time toggle in Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps. Android remembers your choice.

Is it safe to install apps outside the Play Store?

It's safe as long as the source is trustworthy. Sideloading is a normal Android feature — millions of users install APKs directly. openglix APKs are built and published here, contain no trackers, and do not request internet permission unless a feature requires it.

General rule: only install APKs from sites you trust, avoid APKs from random file-sharing links, and check the developer's name.

Google Play Protect says the app is unsafe — is that real?

Play Protect warns about any app it hasn't scanned through the Play Store, even safe ones. This is normal for sideloaded APKs.

If you downloaded the APK from this site, tap Install anyway or More details → Install anyway. Do not proceed if the APK came from anywhere else.

Can I install openglix apps on iPhone or iPad?

No. openglix apps are Android APKs. iOS does not support APK installation, and there is no iOS version available.

The downloaded file won't open — what should I do?

Try these in order:

  • Open the file from the Files app rather than a notification — the notification shortcut sometimes fails.
  • Check the file size against the listing — if it's very small, the download was incomplete. Delete and download again.
  • If your phone shows "Can't open file", install a file manager app like Material Files or Google Files first.
  • If it still fails, the APK may be incompatible with your Android version — check the compatibility notes above.
Do the apps need internet?

No. The apps are 100% offline by design. Every feature — income and expense tracking, loans, reminders, PDF reports — works without internet. Internet is only needed to download the APK or optionally upload a backup to Google Drive.

Is there a Nepali or multilingual version?

Yes. booksXR v1.1.1 added full English and Nepali (नेपाली) support. You can switch languages in the app settings. More languages will be added if there's demand — contact us if you need a specific one.

How do I create an income or expense entry?

Open the app → tap the + button on the home screen → choose Income or Expense → enter the amount, category, date, and optional note → tap Save. Entries appear in your list and are reflected in your balance immediately.

Can I export my data as PDF?

Yes. Go to Settings → Export and choose the date range. The app generates a PDF report with a category breakdown, totals, and the full list of entries. You can share it via any app on your phone.

What if I forget my PIN?

If you have fingerprint unlock enabled, use it. Otherwise, there's no way to recover a PIN without losing access to encrypted data — this is by design to keep your data private.

Uninstalling the app and reinstalling will reset it, but also erases your data. Always keep a backup exported somewhere safe.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in the app's private database. It's not stored in the cloud, and it's only accessible from the app itself (and by you, on your own device).

Is my data sent to openglix servers?

No. openglix apps do not send your data anywhere. There is no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. The only outbound network requests the app ever makes are to Google Drive (if you explicitly choose to back up there) — and even that is optional.

Can you see my financial data?

No. Data lives exclusively on your device. Even if you emailed us a bug report, we have no way to view your entries — we would need you to export and share the report yourself.

How do I protect my data with a PIN or fingerprint?

Open Settings → Security inside the app. You can set a numeric PIN, enable fingerprint unlock, and configure an auto-lock timer (for example, lock after 1 minute of inactivity). These features are included in booksXR v1.1.1 and later.

How do I delete all my data?

Two options:

  • In the app: Settings → Advanced → Clear all data. This wipes entries and settings but keeps the app installed.
  • Uninstall the app: removing the app from your phone deletes the database permanently.

Either way, the data is gone — back it up first if you might need it.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the new APK from the Downloads page, then tap it. Android installs the new version directly over the old one — no need to uninstall first. The update keeps your existing data intact.

Will updating delete my data?

No. Installing a new APK over the same app (same package name) preserves all data — entries, settings, PIN, everything. Only an uninstall removes data.

How do I back up my data?

Two options inside the app:

  • Export: Settings → Export creates a file (JSON or PDF) you can save to Google Drive or email to yourself.
  • Encrypted backup: Settings → Backup creates a password-protected backup file. Store it in Drive or on your computer.

Either way, back up before uninstalling or switching phones.

Can I transfer my data to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup from the old phone (Settings → Backup), copy it to Google Drive or a USB cable, then install the app on the new phone and use Settings → Restore to import it. The password (if you set one) will be required.

How often are updates released?

Updates are released when there's something meaningful to ship — bug fixes, new features, or security improvements. There's no fixed schedule. Check the Updates & Blog page or the Status page for the latest version.

App won't install — what do I do?

Check in this order:

  • Did you enable "Install from unknown sources" for your browser? (See Installation above.)
  • Is there enough free storage? Apps need ~50 MB free.
  • Is the file complete? Compare its size with the listing.
  • Is it compatible? Android 8.0+ is required.
  • Still stuck? Report the issue with your phone model and the exact error message.
App opens then closes immediately

This is usually caused by a corrupt database or an interrupted update. Try:

  1. Force stop the app: Settings → Apps → booksXR → Force stop.
  2. Clear cache (not data): Settings → Apps → booksXR → Storage → Clear cache.
  3. If it still crashes, back up data from within the app if possible, then reinstall.

If the problem persists, report it — include your phone model and Android version.

Reminders aren't firing on time

On phones with aggressive battery optimization (Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung), reminders may be delayed or blocked. Try:

  • Update to booksXR v1.1.1 or later — it fixed a common reminder-timing bug.
  • In phone settings, disable battery optimization for booksXR: Settings → Battery → App launch → booksXR → Allow background activity.
  • On Xiaomi/MIUI: enable Autostart and set battery saver to No restrictions.
The app shows a "?" instead of a minus sign

This was a font bug in booksXR v1.1.0. It's fixed in v1.1.1 and later. Download the latest version to resolve it.

Where can I report a bug?

Use the Report a Problem page. Include:

  • Phone model (e.g. Redmi Note 12)
  • Android version (e.g. Android 13)
  • App name and version
  • What you were trying to do, and what went wrong

That's enough for us to investigate quickly.
