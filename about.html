About openglix

openglix is a software development company. We design and build mobile, desktop, and web applications — for our own products and for clients who need something custom-built.

Our focus is straightforward: practical software that works reliably, respects the user, and is easy to maintain over time. Whether it’s an app we ship ourselves or one we build for a client, the same standards apply.

What we do

Mobile apps

Native Android apps, iOS apps, and cross-platform builds for teams that need one codebase across both.

Desktop software

Windows, macOS, and Linux applications — utilities, business tools, and internal systems.

Web apps

Static sites, dashboards, and web applications — from a single landing page to full client portals.

Custom development

Tailored software built to your workflow and requirements, priced per project.

Our aim

To build software that people actually want to use — not bloated, not tied to an account, and not dependent on services that may disappear.

  • Reliable by default. Apps that work offline, keep data safe, and don’t break when the network does.
  • Honest engineering. Clear scope, clear pricing, and clear release notes — no vague promises.
  • Built to last. Software that stays usable across OS versions and devices instead of needing a rewrite every year.
  • Custom when it matters. Ready-made when it doesn’t. We build what the problem actually requires.

How we work

  • Understand the problem first. Every project starts with a clear picture of who will use it and why.
  • Keep it focused. A smaller app that does its job well beats a large one that tries to do everything.
  • Respect the user’s data. Privacy-conscious design, minimal permissions, and local-first storage where it makes sense.
  • Price clearly. Own apps are free to download where possible. Custom development is quoted per project — no hidden fees, no forced subscriptions.
