About openglix

openglix builds small, practical, offline-first Android apps. The goal is simple: useful software that respects your privacy and does not force accounts or subscriptions.

  • Offline-first: Your data stays on your device.
  • Private: No tracking or cloud dependency.
  • Free: No ads and no premium tiers.
