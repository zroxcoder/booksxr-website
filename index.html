How to Install

Takes about 30 seconds. No Google Play Store required.

  1. Download the APK: Tap the download button above. The file will save to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open the file: Tap the downloaded file from your notification bar or Files app.
  3. Allow installation: Android will ask to allow "Install from unknown sources" — turn it on for your browser. This is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  4. Install & open: Tap Install, then Open. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap "Install anyway" — the app is safe.

FAQ & Help

Is it really free?
Yes — no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription. Made for family and community use.

Does it need internet?
No. The app works 100% offline. All your data stays on your phone. You only need internet to download the app or to upload a backup to Google Drive (optional).

Where is my data stored?
On your phone, in a private database. Nothing is sent to us or any server. Only you can see it.

How do I update?
Come back to this site when a new version is available. Download the new APK and tap Install — it will update over the old one and keep all your data.

About booksXR

booksXR was built to solve a real problem for small business owners. Most finance apps require an internet connection, force you to create an account, and charge a monthly subscription.

We wanted an app that just works. A digital business diary that respects your privacy. That is why booksXR is:

  • 100% Offline: Your data never leaves your device.
  • Private: No tracking, no analytics, no cloud.
  • Free: No ads, no premium tiers.
  • Bilingual: Full support for English and नेपाली.
