FAQ

Common questions.

Is it really free?

Yes — no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription. Made for family and community use.

Does it need internet?

No. The app works 100% offline. All your data stays on your phone. You only need internet to download the app or to upload a backup to Google Drive (optional).

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in a private database. Nothing is sent to us or any server. Only you can see it.

How do I update?

Come back to this site when a new version is available. Download the new APK and tap Install — it will update over the old one and keep all your data.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. The app is signed with a consistent signature so Android will verify updates are from the same developer. No harmful code. If you have doubts, run it through VirusTotal before installing.

Can I use it on iPhone?

No — Android only for now. iPhone users cannot install APK files.
